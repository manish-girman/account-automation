## Decided so far

We are an AI-native CA firm. Services as software, high revenue per person. That rules out selling software to other firms, and rules out shipping an ERPNext add-on.

A partner CA signs, not Sumit, paid per signature by work type. Signing capacity becomes something we buy rather than a fixed ceiling.

## What those two decisions cost us

Per-signature pay makes review time a cost to the signer, so we are paying someone to sign quickly and not look. Track the share of filings the signer sends back. If that number goes to zero, the review is not real.

Revenue per person is clients per person times fee per client, and automation only moves the first number. At a ₹10,000 monthly fee we need roughly 42 clients per person. At ₹25,000, roughly 17. Those are different businesses and we have not picked one.

## The scope problem

Monday's call added inventory with expiry and weight loss, income tax and ITR, statutory audit, Companies Act reports, a tax knowledge base built on rulings and case law, Hindi support, and company formation.

Each addition makes sense on its own. Together they are several years of work, and today we have no clients and an empty repository.

The riskiest one is the tax knowledge base. The call itself noted that case law is often ambiguous. A wrong tax position, signed by a CA who is paid per signature, is the worst thing we could build.
